About Me

I am a student-athlete at University College London, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics, whilst playing semi-profesional ice hockey for the Haringey Huskies. I was born in London, United Kingdom, and moved to spend just under a decade in Prague, Czech Republic before coming back to pursue undergraduate study at UCL. I am a British-Czech-American tri-national, fluent in both English and Czech, and at a B1-B2 level in German. I have learnt to adapt quickly and well to different cultures and academic environments. Balancing academics with sport, I have practiced time management, teamwork, leadership, hard work, and diligence throughout my life.

Most recently, I have completed a summer research project at UCL focused on analytic number theory. I developed a proof for the special case of Dirichlet's theorem on primes congruent to 7 mod 10 using Dirichlet L-series, complex logarithms, and Euler products. I further explored other special cases as well as the application of Dirichlet's theorem to the distribution of primes with my group, culminating in a presentation of methods and results to peers and faculty advisors at UCL. My academic interests lie primarily in abstract algebra, analytic/algebraic number theory, and cryptography, all of which I am excited to continue pursuing during my final year at UCL.
